Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZORA Lifestyle ("ZORA", "we", "our") handles information in connection with this website and the ZORA Pinterest integration operated by the account owner.

1. Information handled

If the account owner connects a Pinterest account, the integration may access information permitted by the authorization granted to it, such as Pinterest account identity, boards, Pins and related performance analytics. The integration is intended for the owner's own account and content-management activities.

When you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address and message contents.

2. How information is used

Information accessed through Pinterest is used to connect and identify the authorized account, organize the owner's content workflow, create or manage Pins when separately approved by the owner, and review available performance analytics. Contact information is used to respond to enquiries.

3. Authorization and security

Pinterest access is initiated only after the account owner chooses to authorize the integration. Credentials and tokens are handled by the ZORA application using its configured protected local storage. No password or access token should be sent to us by email.

4. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell personal information. Pinterest handles information exchanged with its platform under Pinterest's own privacy terms. This website may link to third-party websites; their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

5. Retention, revocation and deletion requests

Information is retained only as needed for the account owner's content-management and record-keeping purposes. The account owner can disconnect the Pinterest integration or revoke its access through Pinterest account settings. For a privacy or deletion request relating to ZORA, contact us at the email below and identify the information or request clearly.

6. Children's privacy

This service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or integration changes. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
